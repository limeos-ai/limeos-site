Escalations

Two ways a customer gets escalated.Both end with someone reaching out.

The first happens the moment a conversation turns. The second is the end-of-day routine we built for a residential painting company: everyone with a crew on site hears from the team before five.

Right away

When a conversation turns

  1. Flagged

    A text, email or call scores low, brings up a review or a refund, or is the second message nobody answered.

  2. Texted

    The job's PM and the owner get a text with the customer's exact words and the job they belong to.

  3. Picked up

    Whoever takes it says so with one tap, so two people don't call the same customer.

  4. Resolved

    Once the customer is taken care of. The job's score shows whether it worked.

Every weekday

When nobody has reached them

  1. 4:00 PM

    Each PM gets a text listing their customers with a crew on site today, or starting the next working day, that nobody on the team has reached.

  2. 4 to 5 PM

    Any text, email or call from the team takes a customer off the list. Nobody has to mark anything.

  3. 5:00 PM

    Each PM gets a close-out. The owner gets one text with everyone still waiting, the PM named on each line.

  4. 7:00 AM

    The morning digest: open escalations, who is waiting on a reply, and the lowest scores.

What the texts say

To Marisol, PM · 4:00 PM

Hi Marisol. Nobody on the team has reached this customer today: Dana Fairbanks, J-2041. Andre's crew is on site today.

Please reach out before 5:00 PM. Anyone still on the list then goes to the owner.

To Wendell, PM · 5:00 PM

Wendell, Desmond Varga still hasn't heard from us today. That one goes to the owner now.

To the owner · 5:00 PM

1 customer nobody on the team reached today: Desmond Varga, J-2036, PM Wendell T. Emil's crew starts Monday.

  • Weekdays only, and each step runs once a day.
  • If calls or email haven't synced, nothing goes out, so nobody gets a wrong list.
  • The customer calling in doesn't count. Someone on the team has to reach out.
  • A job without a PM goes to the office instead.

Scoring

Calls, texts and emails.Scored from 0 to 100.

Calls

Recorded calls on your phone system are transcribed and matched to the job by the customer's number.

Texts

Every text thread with a customer, on the numbers your office already uses.

Email

The office and estimates inboxes, matched to the job by the customer's address.

What each conversation is read for

Tone
How the customer sounds in their latest message.
Urgency
How soon they need an answer.
Resolution risk
Whether the problem gets solved without someone stepping in.
Review risk
Whether they've brought up a review, a refund, a chargeback or a lawyer.

What a job's score means

  • At risk0 to 39
  • Watch40 to 64
  • Healthy65 to 100

A job's score starts at its first conversation and moves halfway toward each new one. A bad week shows up fast, and so does a problem that got fixed.
